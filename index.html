💡 Insider tips for first-timers

  • Use the Delhi Metro. Get a Tourist Card or the “Delhi Metro Sarathi”/ONDC-style QR tickets in the app; avoid road traffic 8–11 am and 5–9 pm.
  • Best season: October–March. April–June is very hot — visit monuments at sunrise, malls and museums at noon.
  • Museums & monuments: most museums close on Mondays; many monuments are free on special days. Carry ID and cash for small tickets.
  • Gurugram & Noida: use Rapid Metro (Gurugram) and the Aqua Line (Noida/Greater Noida); the Blue Line links Noida to Central Delhi.
  • Day-trip dream: Naini Lake (Nainital, Uttarakhand) is ~300 km away — outside NCR, but a classic weekend getaway from Delhi.
  • Safety & manners: use app cabs/auto meters, dress modestly at religious sites, and bargain politely in markets.
